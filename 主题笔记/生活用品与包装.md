# 生活用品与包装

## 包装与容器

- 一包、一小袋：`a pack of cookies` / `a pack of tissues` / `a packet of sugar`
- 六罐／六瓶一组：`a six-pack of beer` / `a six-pack of bottled water`
- 包裹：`package` / `parcel` / `packet`
- 快递袋：`poly mailer`
- 容器盖：`lid` / `cap` / `flap`
- 胶带：`packing tape` / `peel the tape off`

## 日常用品

- 纸巾：`tissue` / `wet wipes` / `dry wipes`
- 台灯：`desk lamp` / `table lamp`
- 快递、快递站：`get the delivery` / `parcel pickup point`
- 剪刀、指甲刀：`a pair of scissors` / `a pair of nail clippers`
- 一次性手套：`disposable gloves` / `a pair of disposable gloves`
- 快递柜：`package locker` / `parcel locker`
- 用完与存货不足：`fresh out of ...` / `run out of ...` / `be short on ...`
