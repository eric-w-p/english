# 日常交流

- 让一下：`Excuse me.` / `Could you let me through?` / `Can I get past?`
- 排队、插队：`wait in line` / `cut in line` / `Don't cut in line.`
- 偶遇熟人：`bump into` / `run into`
- 给你、找到了：`Here you are.` / `There you are.` / `There you go.`
- 出去玩：`hang out` / `go out`
