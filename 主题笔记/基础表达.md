# 基础表达

## 数量

- 一些、几个：`some` / `a few`
- 几乎没有：`few`
- 房号里的 0：`Unit 1203`（读作 `unit twelve oh three`）

## 时间

- 10 点后的分钟：`ten ten` / `ten fifteen` / `ten thirty` / `ten forty-five` / `ten fifty`
- 半点、一刻及差几分：`half past ten` / `a quarter past ten` / `a quarter to eleven` / `ten to eleven`

## 常用句型

- 偏好：`prefer A to B` / `prefer doing something`
- 比较两种做法：`prefer doing A to doing B` / `prefer to do A rather than do B`
- 一般偏好与当前选择：`Do you prefer A or B?` / `Would you prefer A or B?`
- 还想做某事：`Do you still want to ...?`
- 一点点：`a tiny bit` / `a tiny little bit`

## 性质与范围

- 私人的、私密的、非公共的：`private`
- 私下地；不公开：`in private` / `keep ... private`
- 正式提交或归档、与“填写”区分：`filed a complaint` / `filed the receipts` / `filled out a form`
- 格式、排版：`file format` / `format a document` / `formatting`
- 产生与复现：`produce` / `reproduce a bug`

## 人物与奖项

- 金牌、银牌：`gold medal` / `silver medal`
- 模特：`model` / `fashion model`
