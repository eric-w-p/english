# 身体与护理

## 身体感受与受伤

- 肚子、胃：`stomach` / `belly` / `tummy`
- 蚊子包和痒：`mosquito bite` / `itchy` / `itch`
- 感冒和流鼻涕：`have a cold` / `have a runny nose` / `blow my nose`
- 受伤、膝盖疼：`get hurt` / `hurt my knee` / `sore`
- 扭伤：`twist your ankle`
- 胃痛、胃不舒服：`have a stomachache` / `have an upset stomach`
- 手指、脚趾：`finger` / `toe`
- 每只手五根手指、每只脚五个脚趾：`five fingers on each hand` / `five toes on each foot`
- 高血压、测血压：`high blood pressure` / `check your blood pressure` / `take someone's blood pressure`
- 外部压力与紧绷感：`put pressure on someone` / `be under pressure` / `feel stressed` / `cause stress`

## 护理与饮食

- 吹干头发：`blow-dry your hair`
- 擦干、挂毛巾：`dry ... with a towel` / `hang the towel up`
- 剪指甲：`clip your nails` / `trim your nails`
- 护手霜：`hand cream`
- 遵医嘱开始低盐饮食或用药：`put someone on a low-salt diet` / `put someone on medication`
- 减少碳水摄入：`cut down on carbs`
- 肌肉与蛋白质：`muscle` / `protein` / `build muscle` / `get enough protein`
