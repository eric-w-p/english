## 1. 食物、饮品与餐具

> 关联主题：[[主题笔记/餐饮]]

### 猪肉干、牛肉干、布丁、培根

- 表达：pork jerky / beef jerky / pudding / bacon
- 例句：
  - I bought a bag of beef jerky for the trip.（我买了一袋牛肉干，旅行时吃。）
  - Would you like some pudding for dessert?（你想吃点布丁当甜点吗？）
  - I had bacon and eggs for breakfast.（我早餐吃了培根和鸡蛋。）

### 芝士、奶酪、奶酪片、奶酪碎

- 表达：cheese / a slice of cheese / shredded cheese / cream cheese
- 例句：
  - Would you like cheese on your burger?（你的汉堡要加芝士吗？）
  - Put a slice of cheese on the bread.（在面包上放一片奶酪。）
  - Sprinkle some shredded cheese on top.（在上面撒一些奶酪碎。）

### 番茄酱、几包番茄酱

- 表达：ketchup / tomato ketchup / a packet of ketchup / a sachet of ketchup
- 例句：
  - Could I get a few more packets of ketchup, please?（能再给我几包番茄酱吗？）
  - Do you want ketchup with your fries?（你的薯条要配番茄酱吗？）

### 吃饭的勺子、厨房用勺、汤勺、叉子、刀、舀汤

- 表达：spoon / cooking spoon / serving spoon / ladle /ˈleɪdəl/ / scoop up some soup / ladle the soup into a bowl / fork / knife / cutlery
- 例句：
  - Here's your spoon.（你的勺子，给你。）
  - Scoop up some soup with a spoon.（用勺子舀一些汤。）
  - Ladle the soup into a bowl.（用汤勺把汤盛进碗里。）
  - Could I have a knife and fork, please?（请给我一副刀叉，可以吗？）

### 餐具、菜肴、洗碗、堆积

- 表达：dish / dishes / do the dishes / pile / a pile of dishes / pile up / be piled up
- 例句：
  - The dishes are piled up in the sink.（盘子和餐具堆在水槽里。）
  - There's a pile of dirty dishes in the sink.（水槽里有一堆脏餐具。）
  - I'll do the dishes after dinner.（晚饭后我来洗碗。）

### 薯条、炸鸡、炒饭

- 表达：fries / French fries / fried chicken / fried rice
- 例句：
  - Can I get a large order of fries?（我可以要一大份薯条吗？）
  - We ordered fried chicken and egg fried rice.（我们点了炸鸡和蛋炒饭。）

### 中午吃什么

- 表达：What should we have for lunch? / What are you having for lunch? / What do you want for lunch? / What's for lunch?
- 例句：
  - What should we have for lunch today?（我们今天中午吃什么？）
  - What are you having for lunch?（你午饭吃什么？）
  - What do you feel like having for lunch?（你中午想吃点什么？）

### 从牛奶盒上取下吸管、拆开包装并插进去

- 表达：straw / milk carton / peel the straw off / unwrap the straw / tear open the wrapper / tear ... off / stick the straw in / push it through the opening
- 例句：
  - Peel the straw off the milk carton, tear open the wrapper, and stick the straw in.（把吸管从牛奶盒上揭下来，撕开包装，再把吸管插进去。）
  - Unwrap the straw and push it through the opening.（拆掉吸管包装，把它插进开口。）

### 把牛奶倒进咖啡里、给咖啡加牛奶

- 表达：pour some milk into the coffee / add milk to the coffee / take milk in coffee
- 例句：
  - Pour some milk into the coffee and stir it.（往咖啡里倒些牛奶，然后搅拌一下。）
  - Would you like to add some milk to your coffee?（你想在咖啡里加点牛奶吗？）


## 2. 日常交流

> 关联主题：[[主题笔记/日常交流]]

### 让一下、让我过一下

- 表达：Excuse me. / Could you let me through? / Can I get past? / Let me pass. / make way
- 例句：
  - Excuse me, could you let me through?（不好意思，能让我过一下吗？）
  - Can I get past, please?（可以让我过去吗？）
  - Please let me pass.（请让我过去。）

### 排队、插队、别插队

- 表达：line up / wait in line / queue / cut in line / jump the queue / Don't cut in line.
- 例句：
  - We had to wait in line for 20 minutes.（我们排了20分钟的队。）
  - Excuse me, the line starts back there.（不好意思，队尾在那边。）
  - Please don't cut in line.（请不要插队。）

### 偶遇熟人

- 表达：bump into / run into / run into someone I know
- 例句：
  - I ran into someone I know at the mall.（我在商场偶遇了一个熟人。）
  - I bumped into an old friend yesterday.（我昨天偶遇了一位老朋友。）


## 3. 出行与人流

> 关联主题：[[主题笔记/交通]]

### 人群、拥挤、高峰期、非高峰期、错峰出行

- 表达：crowd / crowded / rush hour / peak hours / off-peak hours / outside of rush hour / travel during off-peak times
- 例句：
  - Tourist attractions are usually very crowded during public holidays.（景区在节假日期间通常非常拥挤。）
  - I try to commute outside of rush hour.（我尽量错开交通高峰通勤。）
  - We travel during off-peak times to avoid the crowds.（我们错峰出行，以避开人群。）

### 单程、往返通勤与车票

- 表达：one-way commute / round-trip commute / commute to and from work / one-way ticket / round-trip ticket / return ticket
- 例句：
  - My one-way commute takes about 30 minutes.（我单程通勤大约需要30分钟。）
  - I commute to and from work by subway.（我坐地铁上下班。）
  - I'd like a round-trip ticket, please.（我想买一张往返票。）

### 有轨电车、轻轨

- 表达：tram / streetcar / light rail / take the tram
- 例句：
  - We took the tram to the city center.（我们乘有轨电车去了市中心。）
  - You can get there by light rail.（你可以乘轻轨到那里。）

### 通过地铁站闸机、刷卡进站

- 表达：go through the subway gate / ticket gate / fare gate / turnstile / tap your card
- 例句：
  - Tap your card and go through the fare gate.（刷卡后通过闸机。）
  - I couldn't get through the subway gate.（我没能通过地铁闸机。）

### 等红灯、过马路、在人行横道处、闯红灯

- 表达：wait for the light to turn green / wait at the crosswalk / cross the street / at the crosswalk / run a red light / cross against the light
- 例句：
  - Wait at the crosswalk until the light turns green.（在人行横道处等绿灯亮起。）
  - Look both ways before you cross the street.（过马路前要看看两边。）
  - Don't run a red light.（不要闯红灯。）
  - He crossed the street against the light.（他闯红灯过了马路。）

### 交通事故、车祸、发生事故

- 表达：traffic accident / car accident / crash / get into an accident
- 例句：
  - There was a traffic accident on my way to work.（我上班路上发生了一起交通事故。）
  - They got into a car accident, but no one was hurt.（他们出了车祸，但没有人受伤。）
  - The crash caused a long traffic jam.（这起事故造成了长时间堵车。）

### 撞上、轻微碰到

- 表达：crash into / bump into
- 例句：
  - The car crashed into a tree.（汽车撞上了一棵树。）
  - I bumped into a chair in the dark.（我在黑暗中撞到了椅子。）

### 开车送老婆上班、骑电动车带老婆拿快递、下楼拿快递、快递站

- 表达：drive my wife to work / give my wife a ride / take my wife on my electric scooter / go downstairs to pick up my package / get the delivery / parcel pickup point
- 例句：
  - I drove my wife to work this morning.（今天早上我开车送老婆去上班。）
  - I took my wife to the parcel pickup point on my electric scooter to pick up a package.（我骑电动车带老婆去快递站拿快递。）
  - I'm going downstairs to pick up my package.（我要下楼拿快递。）
