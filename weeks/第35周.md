## 短语

### 做家务

- 表达：do the housework / do chores
- 例句：
  - I usually do the housework on weekends.（我通常周末做家务。）

### 扫地

- 表达：sweep the floor
- 例句：
  - Can you help me sweep the floor?（你能帮我扫一下地吗？）

### 拖地

- 表达：mop the floor
- 例句：
  - I need to mop the floor. It's a little dirty.（我得拖一下地，有点脏了。）

### 擦桌子

- 表达：wipe the table
- 例句：
  - Could you wipe the table after dinner?（晚饭后你能擦一下桌子吗？）

### 整理、收好玩具

- 表达：tidy up the toys / put away the toys
- 例句：
  - Let's tidy up the toys.（我们把玩具整理一下吧。）
  - Please put away your toys when you're done.（玩完后请把玩具收好。）

### 早睡；感觉精神焕发

- 表达：go to bed early / feel refreshed
- 例句：
  - I went to bed early last night, so I feel really refreshed today.（我昨天睡得早，所以今天感觉很精神。）

### 工作永远做不完

- 表达：The work never ends. / There's always more work to do.
- 例句：
  - The work never ends around here.（这里的工作永远做不完。）
  - There's always more work to do.（总有更多工作要做。）

### 太咸；口渴；一瓶无糖可乐

- 表达：too salty / thirsty / a bottle of sugar-free cola
- 例句：
  - The egg and cucumber noodles I had today were too salty. I'm really thirsty, and I want a bottle of sugar-free cola.（我今天吃的鸡蛋黄瓜面太咸了。我很渴，想喝一瓶无糖可乐。）

### 厨师放了太多盐

- 表达：The chef added too much salt. / add too much salt
- 例句：
  - The chef added too much salt to the noodles, so they were really salty.（厨师在面里放了太多盐，所以面很咸。）

### 天气有风、凉快

- 表达：windy / breezy / cool / chilly / muggy
- 例句：
  - It's windy outside today, and it feels nice and cool.（今天外面有风，感觉很凉快。）
  - It's nice and breezy today.（今天微风习习，很舒服。）
  - It's a little chilly this morning.（今天早晨有点冷。）
  - It's hot and muggy outside.（外面又热又闷。）

### 薯片、薯条与快餐套餐

- 表达：potato chips / fries / cucumber-flavored chips / a KFC combo / come with
- 例句：
  - I ordered a KFC combo. It came with medium fries, a medium Coke, and a double beef burger.（我点了一份肯德基套餐，里面有一份中薯、一杯中杯可乐和一个双层牛肉汉堡。）
  - These cucumber-flavored chips are really good.（这些黄瓜味薯片很好吃。）

### 吃得很饱、感到内疚、担心变胖

- 表达：be full / be stuffed / feel guilty / gain weight
- 例句：
  - I'm stuffed. I feel guilty for eating so much. I'm going to gain weight if I keep eating like this.（我吃得太饱了。吃了这么多，我有点罪恶感。再这样吃下去，我要变胖了。）

### 饭后在公司附近走走

- 表达：after lunch / go for a walk / near my office
- 例句：
  - After lunch, I usually go for a walk near my office.（吃完午饭，我通常会在公司楼下附近走走。）
  - Let's take a short walk after lunch.（午饭后我们走一小会儿吧。）

### 刚下班

- 表达：I just got off work. / I've just finished work.
- 例句：
  - I just got off work. I'm on my way home now.（我刚下班，现在正在回家。）
  - I've just finished work. Do you want to grab dinner?（我刚下班，要不要一起吃晚饭？）

### 食品杂货、买菜

- 表达：groceries / grocery store / go grocery shopping / pick up some groceries / grocery list
- 例句：
  - I need to stop by the grocery store and pick up some groceries on my way home.（我回家路上得去一趟食品杂货店，买点吃的和日用品。）
  - Don't forget to make a grocery list.（别忘了列一张购物清单。）

### 扫码支付、刷卡支付

- 表达：scan the QR code to pay / pay by card / pay with a card / swipe your card
- 例句：
  - You can scan the QR code to pay.（你可以扫码支付。）
  - Can I pay by card?（我可以刷卡支付吗？）
  - Please swipe your card here.（请在这里刷卡。）

### 手机充电与电量

- 表达：charge my phone / charging cable / charger / be at 70% / be dead / be about to die / be running low
- 例句：
  - I need to charge my phone. Do you have a charger or a charging cable?（我得给手机充电。你有充电器或充电线吗？）
  - My phone is still at 70%.（我的手机还有 70% 的电。）
  - My phone is dead.（我的手机没电了。）
  - My phone is about to die.（我的手机快没电了。）

### 用完；车快没油了；去加油

- 表达：run out of / be running out of gas / be running low on gas / get gas / fill up
- 例句：
  - The car is running out of gas. I need to stop and get gas.（车快没油了，我得停下来加油。）
  - We're running low on gas. Let's fill up at the next gas station.（我们的油不多了，去下一个加油站加满吧。）
  - We've run out of milk.（我们的牛奶喝完了。）

### 耳机、无线耳机、蓝牙耳机、头戴式耳机

- 表达：earphones / earbuds / wireless earbuds / Bluetooth earbuds / headphones / over-ear headphones
- 例句：
  - I use wireless earbuds when I'm outside and over-ear headphones at home.（我出门时用无线耳机，在家用头戴式耳机。）
  - My Bluetooth earbuds aren't connecting to my phone.（我的蓝牙耳机连不上手机。）

### 电梯、自动扶梯、乘电梯上楼或下楼

- 表达：elevator / lift / escalator / take the elevator up or down
- 例句：
  - Let's take the elevator up to the fifth floor.（我们乘电梯上五楼吧。）
  - Take the elevator down to the lobby.（乘电梯下到大堂。）
  - We can take the escalator upstairs.（我们可以乘自动扶梯上楼。）

### 零钱、正好的零钱、不用找零

- 表达：change / loose change / exact change / keep the change
- 例句：
  - Do you have any change?（你有零钱吗？）
  - You need exact change for the bus.（坐公交车需要准备正好的零钱。）
  - Keep the change.（不用找了。）

### 零食、吃点零食

- 表达：snack / snacks / grab a snack / snack on something
- 例句：
  - I'm a little hungry. I'm going to grab a snack.（我有点饿了，准备吃点零食。）
  - I like to snack on fruit in the afternoon.（我喜欢下午吃点水果当零食。）

### 更喜欢、宁愿

- 表达：prefer A to B / prefer doing something / would prefer to do something
- 例句：
  - I prefer coffee to tea.（比起茶，我更喜欢咖啡。）
  - I prefer walking to driving.（比起开车，我更喜欢走路。）
  - I'd prefer to stay home tonight.（今晚我更想待在家里。）

### 给你；好了；找到了

- 表达：Here you are. / There you are. / There you go.
- 例句：
  - “Can I see the menu?” “Sure, here you are.”（“我能看看菜单吗？”“当然，给你。”）
  - There you are! I've been looking for you.（原来你在这儿！我一直在找你。）
  - There you go—your phone is working again.（好了，你的手机又能用了。）

### 一点点；小小的

- 表达：a tiny bit / a tiny little bit / a tiny little + 名词
- 例句：
  - I'm a tiny bit tired.（我有一点点累。）
  - Can you move it a tiny little bit?（你能把它稍微挪一点吗？）
  - They have a tiny little kitchen.（他们有一个小小的厨房。）

### 可以……吗；点餐

- 表达：Can I ...? / Can I have ...? / Can I get ...? / I'd like ...
- 例句：
  - Can I get a cheeseburger and a medium Coke, please?（请给我一个芝士汉堡和一杯中杯可乐，可以吗？）
  - Can I have the chicken combo, please?（我可以要一份鸡肉套餐吗？）
  - I'd like a cup of coffee, please.（我想要一杯咖啡。）

### 出去玩、和朋友待在一起

- 表达：hang out / hang out with someone / go out / go out with someone
- 例句：
  - I'm going to hang out with some friends this weekend.（这周末我要和几个朋友出去玩。）
  - Do you want to go out tonight?（你今晚想出去玩吗？）
  - We used to hang out all the time.（我们以前经常一起玩。）

### 筷子、一双筷子、使用筷子

- 表达：chopsticks / a pair of chopsticks / use chopsticks / eat with chopsticks
- 例句：
  - Could I have a pair of chopsticks, please?（请给我一双筷子，可以吗？）
  - Do you know how to use chopsticks?（你会用筷子吗？）
  - I usually eat noodles with chopsticks.（我通常用筷子吃面。）

### 还想做某事、还想去高邮玩

- 表达：Do you still want to ...? / Do you want to ... again? / visit Gaoyou
- 例句：
  - Do you still want to go?（你还想去吗？）
  - Do you still want to visit Gaoyou?（你还想去高邮玩吗？）
  - Would you like to visit Gaoyou again?（你想再去一次高邮吗？）

### 去剪头、理发店、理发师、花了30元

- 表达：get a haircut / barbershop / hair salon / barber / hairdresser / cost 30 yuan
- 例句：
  - I went to the barbershop to get a haircut. It cost me 30 yuan.（我去理发店剪了头发，花了30元。）
  - The barber cut my hair really short.（理发师把我的头发剪得很短。）

### 速溶饮品

- 表达：instant beverage / instant drink / instant coffee / powdered drink mix
- 例句：
  - Instant coffee is quick and easy to make.（速溶咖啡冲泡起来又快又方便。）
  - Just add hot water to make this instant drink.（这种速溶饮品加热水冲泡就行。）
